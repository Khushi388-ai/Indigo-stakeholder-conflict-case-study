# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Wants | Fears | Conflicts with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affected passengers | Early, accurate information; a fast, fair remedy | Being stranded with no information; losing money or an important trip | Finance (refund speed versus cost control) |
| Pilots and cabin crew | Advance notice of rosters; duty within legal limits | Being pushed past legal rest limits | Operations control and leadership |
| Operations control | Enough legal crew; flexibility to re-plan | Running out of legal crew with no time to react | Crew and DGCA |
| Customer care and airport staff | Clear information, tools and authority | Facing angry passengers with nothing to offer | Finance (authority versus limits) |
| Finance and refunds | Controlled, trackable cost | Large unplanned cash outflow; fraud | Passengers and customer care |
| DGCA (regulator) | Safe, compliant operations; passengers treated properly | Rules ignored under commercial pressure | Leadership |
| Leadership and crisis group | Protect reputation, revenue and schedule | Loss of trust; regulatory action | Operations control; DGCA |
