# Elicitation Questions

| # | Stakeholder | Question | This helps me decide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Passengers | How did you find out your flight was cancelled (SMS, app, airport, other), and how many hours before departure? | Notification channel and timing |
| 2 | Passengers | After the cancellation, what did you try first to rebook or get a refund, and what stopped you from completing it? | Which self-service steps to fix first |
| 3 | Crew | In the two weeks before 3 December, how often did your roster change with less than 24 hours' notice? | Minimum roster notice |
| 4 | Crew | When your duty hours approached the legal limit, what information did you receive, from whom, and when? | Duty-limit alert requirement |
| 5 | Operations control | What share of crew was held in reserve each day before the new rules, and what is it now? | Reserve-crew buffer |
| 6 | Operations control | When available crew fell below what the schedule needed, what steps did you take in order, and when was the decision to cancel made? | Escalation and cancellation trigger |
| 7 | Customer care | In the first 48 hours, what were the 5 most common reasons passengers contacted you, with counts? | Which features and notifications to prioritise |
| 8 | Customer care | What did passengers ask for that you could not give them, and what was missing (data, tool or authority)? | Information and authority requirements |

I chose these four stakeholders because they follow the failure chain: capacity problem, the decision to cancel, passenger information, then handling.
