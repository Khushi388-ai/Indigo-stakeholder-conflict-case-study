# IndiGo December 2025 Disruption: Stakeholder Conflicts and Prioritisation

**Day 2 of a 15-day Business Analysis Case Study Challenge** | Author: Khushi Singh
Skill shown: stakeholder conflicts, requirement prioritisation (MoSCoW) and a trade-off decision.

Independent learning project using public information only. Not affiliated with IndiGo. Internal roles, processes and thresholds are assumptions and are marked as such. Verify figures against the sources listed below.

## 1. The case in brief
- In December 2025 IndiGo cancelled flights amid crew shortages and new Flight Duty Time Limitation (FDTL) rules.
- Reported on-time performance fell from 84% in October to 68% in November, with over 1,200 cancellations in November. The peak day saw more than 550 cancellations against roughly 2,300 flights a day.
- In the first week IndiGo reported about Rs 827 crore in refunds and over 9,500 hotel rooms arranged.
- The DGCA said mandatory passenger information and facilities were not provided. The board formed a crisis management group.

## 2. Problem, goal and scope
**Problem:** New duty-time rules met a thin crew buffer. Flights were cancelled, passengers were told late, and refunds and care strained customer care and finance.
**Goal:** show how stakeholder conflicts decide what is built first.
**In scope:** crew planning, passenger notification, rebooking and refunds, customer care tools.
**Out of scope:** fleet, pricing, safety investigations, air traffic issues.
